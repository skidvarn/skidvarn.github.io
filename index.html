About

Skidvarn is a digital space dedicated to audiovisual experimentation and artistic expression. Through the fusion of sound, video, and interactive media, we explore new forms of digital art that challenge perception and engage multiple senses.

This platform showcases experimental works that blend technology with creativity, creating immersive experiences that exist at the boundary of art and code.
